    Questions

    Is my data uploaded anywhere?

    No. Indexing, extraction, and organization all run locally. Optional AI features call a provider you configure in Settings — nothing is sent automatically.

    What happens after the 28 days?

    The app enters read-only mode: browse, search, and export keep working; creating, scanning, renaming, and organizing pause. Activating a Pro license key (when available) restores full use.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Just an invite code you were given. There’s no email sign-up and no lock-in.

    When will new invite codes be issued?

    Periodically, as we grow the beta. The page only needs to be refreshed — new codes validate instantly.
